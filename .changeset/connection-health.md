`doctor --debug-port` and `wake` list the browser tabs on the Penpot origin and which one holds the pinned file. `wake --reload` reloads that one tab and waits for the plugin. Writer-lock errors name the holding run, operation, process and command. `penpair locks` lists local writer locks and `penpair locks clear` removes those whose process is gone.
